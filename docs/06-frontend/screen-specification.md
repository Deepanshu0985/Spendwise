# Screen Specification

## S01 Login
Email, password, login and recovery.

## S02 Register
Full name, email, password and confirmation.

## S03 Dashboard
Summary, charts, merchants, budgets, recurring and insights.

## S04 Transactions
List/table, filters, search, add/edit/delete.

## S05 Accounts
List, create/edit/deactivate, type and currency.

## S06 Statement Upload
Drag/drop, validation, account selection, progress and errors.

## S07 Statement Review
Parsed rows, confidence, duplicates, edits, bulk selection and confirmation.

## S08 Budgets
Create budget, category limits and progress.

## S09 Goals
Create goal, progress and target date.

## S10 Recurring
Detected merchant, frequency, amount, next date, confirm/dismiss.

## S11 AI Assistant
Chat and suggested questions.

## S12 Settings
Profile, currency, timezone, privacy, export, deletion and AI preferences.
